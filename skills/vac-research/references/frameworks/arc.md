# ARC Objection Handling

## Core Concept
Universal objection handling framework. Works with any conversation approach.

## Sequence
1. **Acknowledge:** Show you heard and understand ("I completely understand.")
2. **Reframe:** Shift perspective without dismissing ("That's exactly why...")
3. **Continue:** Move the conversation forward with the next logical step

## Rules
- Max 2 ARC loops per objection. If prospect objects a third time → graceful exit
- Never argue. Never dismiss. Never use "but" or "still"
- The Reframe must add NEW information or perspective, not repeat the pitch
- If the objection is valid (e.g., genuinely no budget): acknowledge and exit gracefully

## Common Objections & Responses

### "Not interested"
- A: "I get that, you probably get a lot of these calls."
- R: "I'm not here to sell you anything — I just have one concrete idea that [specific benefit]."
- C: Continue with value proposition

### "I don't have time"
- A: "Totally fair."
- R: "Can I give you the short version in 30 seconds?"
- C: Ultra-short pitch → CTA

### "Just send me something"
- A: "Happy to."
- R: "So I send you the right thing: [one short qualifying question]"
- C: Gather info → send targeted follow-up

### "We're all set"
- A: "Good to hear."
- R: "The best companies in [industry] tell us that's exactly why [insight]."
- C: Continue with differentiator

### "Data privacy / GDPR"
- A: "Fair point, we take that seriously."
- R: "Everything we do is GDPR-compliant. [specific detail]."
- C: Continue with next topic

## Best For
- ALL voice agents (universal framework)
- Works alongside any primary framework (SPIN, Challenger, Beratungsansatz)

## Key Principle for Voice Agents
Objection handling must feel empathetic, not scripted. The Acknowledge step is critical — rushing past it makes the agent sound robotic.
