# SPIN Selling (Neil Rackham)

## Core Concept
Question-based consultative selling. Guide the prospect to discover their own needs through structured questions.

## Question Sequence
1. **Situation:** Understand current state (minimal — don't interrogate)
2. **Problem:** Identify difficulties, dissatisfaction, pain points
3. **Implication:** Explore consequences of the problem (amplify urgency)
4. **Need-Payoff:** Get prospect to articulate the value of a solution

## Best For
- Complex B2B sales with multiple stakeholders
- High-value, long sales cycles
- Discovery calls where understanding needs is the primary goal
- When the prospect doesn't yet recognize the full scope of their problem

## Not Ideal For
- Short transactional calls (too many questions = interrogation)
- Outbound cold calls where you have < 2 minutes
- Simple appointment booking
- When the agent lacks context to ask good Situation questions

## Key Principles for Voice Agents
- Keep Situation questions to 1-2 max (prospect's time is limited)
- Problem questions are the most valuable — invest here
- Implication questions create urgency without being pushy
- Need-Payoff lets the prospect sell themselves on the solution
- NEVER answer your own questions — wait for the prospect

## Example Sequence (PDL/Recruiting)
- S: "How are you filling open roles right now?"
- P: "What's the hardest part of that?"
- I: "What does it mean for the business when a role sits open for three months?"
- N: "If you had qualified candidates in front of you within a week — what would that change for you?"
