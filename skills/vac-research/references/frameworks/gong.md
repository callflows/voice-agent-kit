# Gong Research — Data-Driven Conversation Patterns

## Key Findings for Voice Agents

### Rapport Building
- Top performers spend the **first 20% of the call** building rapport
- Asking about the prospect's situation before pitching increases close rates

### Talk Ratio
- Ideal ratio: **43% agent, 57% prospect** for discovery calls
- For short outbound: agent talks more but still asks questions
- Monologues > 76 seconds lose the prospect

### Questions
- 11-14 questions per call is optimal for discovery
- For short calls: 3-5 targeted questions
- Question clustering (multiple questions in a row) reduces effectiveness

### Pricing Discussion
- Top performers discuss pricing **later** in the conversation
- Leading with price commoditizes the conversation

### Objection Timing
- Objections early in the call are often reflexive (handle with ARC)
- Objections late in the call are genuine concerns (address seriously)

### Closing
- Successful closers propose **specific** next steps
- "I'll send you a specific proposal" > "Want me to send you something?"

## Best For
- Data-backed principles that apply to ALL voice agents
- Especially valuable for optimizing conversation patterns post-testing

## Key Principles for Voice Agents
- Keep agent turns short (no monologues > 30 seconds in voice)
- Build rapport first, pitch second
- Ask one question at a time, then listen
- Propose specific next steps, never vague CTAs
