# MEDDPICC — Enterprise Sales Qualification

## Core Concept
Qualification framework for complex enterprise deals. Ensures all critical elements are covered before investing in a deal.

## Framework
- **M**etrics: Quantified business impact
- **E**conomic Buyer: Who controls budget
- **D**ecision Criteria: How they evaluate solutions
- **D**ecision Process: Steps to purchase decision
- **P**aper Process: Legal, procurement, compliance
- **I**mplied Pain: Consequences of not solving the problem
- **C**hampion: Internal advocate for your solution
- **C**ompetition: Who else they're evaluating

## Best For
- Enterprise deals (> €50K)
- Long sales cycles with multiple stakeholders
- When qualification is the primary goal of the call
- Complex B2B with procurement processes

## Not Ideal For
- Short outbound calls (too many elements to cover)
- SMB deals (over-engineered)
- First contact cold calls (premature to qualify this deeply)
- Inbound service or booking calls

## Key Principles for Voice Agents
- Use MEDDPICC as a CHECKLIST, not a conversation script
- Most elements should be discovered naturally, not asked directly
- For voice agents: focus on M (Metrics), E (Economic Buyer), I (Implied Pain)
- Other elements (Paper Process, Competition) are better handled by human follow-up
- Voice agent identifies and qualifies; human closes the complex deal

## Adaptation for Short Calls
- Identify Economic Buyer ("Are you the one who signs off on this?")
- Surface Implied Pain ("What happens if that role stays open?")
- Quantify if possible ("What does every month it's unfilled cost you?")
- Leave detailed qualification to the human follow-up
