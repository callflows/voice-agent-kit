# Beratungsansatz (Advisory-First)

## Core Concept
Position the agent as an advisor, not a seller. Lead with value and expertise rather than product features. Show up as a peer, not someone asking for a favor.

## Principles
1. **Lead with insight:** Share something relevant about the prospect's situation
2. **Ask, don't pitch:** Understand before proposing
3. **Be the expert:** Speak with confidence about the domain
4. **Create value in the call itself:** Even if no deal happens, the prospect learned something
5. **Respect their time:** Get to the point quickly

## Best For
- Short outbound calls (< 5 minutes)
- Placement/recruiting calls (pitching candidates, not jobs)
- When SPIN is too complex for the call length
- When the agent represents domain expertise (not just a product)
- Warm leads who already have context

## Not Ideal For
- Complex enterprise sales (too shallow)
- Inbound service calls (not about advising)
- Cold calls to completely unaware prospects (need pattern interrupt first)

## Key Principles for Voice Agents
- Open with why you're calling (respect their time)
- Quickly establish relevance ("I have a candidate who's a real fit for what you're hiring for")
- Ask 1-2 targeted questions (not a discovery session)
- Present value concisely
- Close with clear next step
- If no interest: accept gracefully, leave door open

## Combination with ARC
- When objections come: Acknowledge → Reframe as advisor → Continue with value
- "I hear you, sounds like you're covered right now. That's exactly why it's worth a look — the strong candidates go fast."
