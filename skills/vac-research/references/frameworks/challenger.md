# Challenger Sale (Dixon & Adamson)

## Core Concept
Teach-Tailor-Take Control. Challenge the prospect's assumptions with unique insights, then tailor the message to their situation.

## Three Behaviors
1. **Teach:** Share insights the prospect doesn't have. Reframe how they think about their problem.
2. **Tailor:** Adapt the message to the specific prospect's context, role, and priorities.
3. **Take Control:** Confidently guide the conversation toward a decision.

## Best For
- B2B sales where the agent has genuine market insights
- Complex sales with educated buyers who think they know the solution
- When differentiation is key (commodity markets)
- Mid-to-large deals where changing perspective creates urgency

## Not Ideal For
- Short calls where there's no time to teach
- When the agent doesn't have real insights (becomes hollow)
- Simple transactions or appointment booking
- Inbound service (customers don't want to be challenged when seeking help)

## Key Principles for Voice Agents
- The "Teach" must be a genuine insight, not a sales pitch disguised as teaching
- Tailor requires knowing the prospect's role and context (Layer 3 variables)
- Take Control means steering to CTA confidently, not aggressively
- Combine with Beratungsansatz for shorter calls: lead with one insight, then advise

## Example (PDL)
- Teach: "Did you know 73% of qualified candidates are off the market within 10 days?"
- Tailor: "In your industry, [firma_branche], we're seeing a real shortage, especially for [specific role]."
- Take Control: "Here's what I'd suggest — let's look at how we solve that for you."
